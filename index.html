Before choosing the full tool
A check before your price import.
Use intended selling prices, not supplier purchase costs. There is no margin, tax or currency calculation. Both input files must use the same currency and tax basis.
The paid version handles UTF-8 CSVs with comma, semicolon or tab separators and dot or comma decimals: up to 5 MiB, 25,000 records and 250 columns per file.
The update contains SKU and Regular price for the documented WooCommerce mapping. It is not a Shopify importer, full import validator or store connector.
Real WooCommerce imports and direct offline opening have not been verified. Back up your store and test a small batch on staging. CSV Price Check is independent of WooCommerce.
